A suggestion from a group owner

The Skool About page, built for the phone first

Most people open a Skool group on a phone. On a phone today the About page shows a small video, six tiny thumbnails nobody can read, and no sidebar at all.

These two pages are working demos of the same About page with the same content Skool already allows. Nothing was added. The only change is how the screen is used.

On a laptop each demo sits inside a device frame. On a real phone or tablet it fills the screen.

What is different, for the designers

  1. The video runs the full width of the screen. It is the first thing a visitor sees and it plays in the page.
  2. The six media slots are a swipe row. Each tile is large enough to read, with its title under it. A row of short lines under the tiles shows there are six and which ones are on screen.
  3. The sections live in a tray at the bottom, where the thumb already is. A phone holds five plus More. A tablet holds all seven.
  4. The top bar and the bottom tray get out of the way. Scroll down and they hide. Scroll back up and they return. Each one also has a small arrow to put it away until it is wanted.
  5. The Join button is one short line and sits just above the tray, always in reach.
  6. No sidebar is needed. Public, members, Free and the owner fit in one row under the video.
  7. The tablet follows the phone, not the laptop: one column, the same order, larger type.

Built by Steven James, Steven James Consulting, a Skool group owner. The member numbers in the demos are typed in, and five of the six tiles are placeholders for videos.
