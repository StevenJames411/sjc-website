The one screen to change, for the designers

The Skool app already gets the phone right everywhere else. There is a bar at the top, a tray at the bottom, and the classroom uses the whole screen. The About page is the one screen that does not, and it is where most visitors land.

  1. The video runs the full width of the screen. It is the first thing a visitor sees and it plays in the page.
  2. The six media slots are a swipe row. Each tile is large enough to read, with its title under it. A row of short lines under the tiles shows there are six and which ones are on screen.
  3. Public, members, Free and the owner sit in one row under the video, then the About text. Nothing from the laptop sidebar is needed.
  4. The Join button is one short line, always in reach.
  5. The tablet follows the phone, not the laptop: one column, the same order, larger type.

The demos also show the top bar and the tray hiding as you scroll, and a tray that holds every section. Those are extras. The request is the About page.

Built by Steven James, Steven James Consulting, a Skool group owner. The member numbers in the demos are typed in, and five of the six tiles are placeholders for videos.
